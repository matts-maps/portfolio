I am a GIS and Information Management specialist with 20 years of experience in implementing humanitarian and resilience programming and providing institutional leadership for international donors and a wide range of development organisations. I have provided in-field and remote support for over 200 disaster responses across Africa, the Middle East, Asia, the Caribbean and the Pacific, and through this have engaged extensively with governments, development organizations, NGOs and civil society stakeholders. I have delivered capacity building, training, institutional strengthening and knowledge management initiatives for clients ranging from major international organisations to CSOs and local communities. I have a particular interest in visualisations, accessibility and utilisation of openly available tools and data. I have a deep understanding of international humanitarian systems and actors and am passionate about translating complex information into accessible and engaging tools to reduce the impact of disasters on people and enhance their resilience.
